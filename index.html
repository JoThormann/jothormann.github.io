Feed Profile Designer

Feed Profile Designer

Puts a fermentation plan on a real calendar, before the run starts.

  • Runs in any browser, nothing to install
  • Shows where every phase switch lands in the week
  • Open source, MIT licensed

How it works

A feed profile is planned on paper and executed on a clock. A phase switch that looks reasonable in a spreadsheet can land at 3am, which you tend to discover once the run is already going.

Set the feed phases; constant, linear ramp, exponential, each with its own temperature and substrate concentration, and it returns the pump setpoints, shows the effective substrate concentrations in the vessel, and shows where every change in the process falls in the week. A goal-seek solver can help make adjustments and move changes into the working hours. Expert mode gives you OUR and specific growth rate information.

DIY off-gas sensor

DIY off-gas sensor

Reads what a bioreactor breathes out, continuously, with parts you can easily order.

  • Logs oxygen uptake, CO₂ production and RQ
  • About €500 in parts
  • Every reading to a timestamped CSV

How it works

Every run vents a continuous record of its own metabolism. Reading it normally means an analyser around €6,500, which is why it often goes unread.

An O₂ cell and a CO₂ cell sit in the vent line, a BME280 corrects both to dry gas, and a Teensy 4.0 streams all three to a timestamped CSV. The cells ship with threads and no mounts, so the Y adapter connecting them is printed with an SLA resin 3D printer.
